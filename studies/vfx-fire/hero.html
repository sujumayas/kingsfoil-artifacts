Top-down pixel fire and smoke: tiny algorithms for campfires, spreading fire, smoke and combat fire

The DOOM fire is one byte of heat per pixel and one rule that copies heat upward. This study is that fire's own: torches and campfires, grass fires that spread over tiles with wind and rain, smoke that drifts and hides things, and fire fields that hurt. Each part has a classic answer a few lines long. A number per cell holds the truth and a palette or sprite turns it into pixels. Aimed at Godot 4 on the web and a server-authoritative MMO.
