# Figure plan for vfx-fire (shared by two figure agents)

Figure names are FIXED. Each `<!--FIG name-->…<!--/FIG-->` block lives in the file listed, its demo is registered with `reg('name', …)` in the matching demos file. Markup follows studies/vfx-light/figs*.html exactly (`<figure class="fig" data-demo="name" id="fig-name">`, `.fig-h` with `Fig. @N`, title, `.illu` label when numbers are illustrative, canvases, controls, figcaption). Figures use only facts in studies/vfx-fire/report.md; label illustrative numbers. No images, no game art. Animations via the core.js demo runner (requestAnimationFrame, paused offscreen).

## Agent R (recipe owner): build.py, hero.html, core.js, style.css/study.css, figs1.html + demos1.js, figs3.html + demos3.js
figs1.html / demos1.js
- `hero` (in hero.html; demo in demos1.js): live 320x180 village edge with campfire, torches, a chimney smoke column, and a grass fire spreading with wind; buttons to jump between "Campfire", "Wildfire", "Rain", "Fireball".
- `doomfire`: the DOOM PSX fire with the 37-colour palette, sliders for decay and wind, a toggle to "extinguish" (bottom row to 0), palette strip.
- `ancestors`: three small buffers side by side: Elias cooling map, Lode kernel, Parusel average-minus-1, same seed.
- `agetable`: age-to-colour table fire and Monte Carlo random-pixel update, with a "pixels updated per frame" readout and a fixed-cost toggle.
- `timeline`: the §3 generations, clickable.
- `flameloop`: a drawn 4-frame flame loop from the 4-tone ramp; fps slider (4-12); a row of 8 torches with a "seed phase from tile" toggle (synced vs desynced).
- `loopspeed`: the f = 1.5/sqrt(D) curve (log x), with candle, torch, campfire, house fire marked; a slider moves a live flame whose loop speed follows.
- `ramp`: palette ramp builder with hue shift; palette cycling mode vs drawn mode side by side.
- `embers`: fixed-pool embers and sparks, colour by age through the ramp, wind and gravity sliders, live particle count.
- `puffs`: the Stardew-style chimney puff column (numbers from the report), wind slider.
- `dither`: 4x4 Bayer threshold smoke (17 levels) vs alpha blending vs fill patterns, density slider.
figs3.html / demos3.js
- `score`: the §5 scorecard, sortable, with a radar for the selected row (copy vfx-light's score figure pattern).
- `budget`: the §6d performance budget as a log-scale bar chart with a 16.7 ms frame line.
- `stack` is generated by build.py from §7 (8 layers).

## Agent F: figs2.html + demos2.js only (never edit build.py, core.js, figs1/3, demos1/3)
- `spread`: tile fire CA: ignite chance, moisture, wind direction/strength, diagonal factor; shows square vs round fronts; click to ignite.
- `costchart`: log-scale ms chart, active list vs full sweep (JS and GDScript, 320x180 and 1024x1024) and smoke options (Stam 64x64 / 320x180, advection+decay, smoke CA), 16.7 ms line. Numbers from the report (M4 Pro).
- `percolation`: random fuel density slider; fire crosses the map above the threshold (0.5927 for 4 neighbours, about 0.407 for 8).
- `fc2budget`: Far Cry 2 style budget pool (60 points, 8 per cell), wind dot-product damage, a meter emptying.
- `extinguish`: rain and water on a burning patch: Minecraft-style chance per tick, steam puffs where water meets fire, burn states fresh -> burning -> embers -> ash.
- `netbytes`: bytes per tick for full burning-set diff vs ignition events vs seeded RNG, as a fire front grows (label illustrative sizes).
- `smokefield`: low-res advected smoke field with decay, wind slider, Bayer-dithered rendering, smoke vs steam vs oil colours.
- `smokesight`: smoke blocking line of sight on a grid (opaque cloud count rule), drag the viewer.
- `tibiafield`: Tibia fire field stages over time with damage ticks (20 + 7x10), showing both timing readings (wiki 9 s vs server code 10 s).
- `burnstatus`: burn models side by side: Terraria (4 HP/s in 1-per-0.25 s ticks), Gungeon (4 DPS for 4 s), Mewgenics (stacks, minus 1 per turn), Pokémon (1/16 per turn): HP curves over time plus a creature sprite with flame overlay and tint flash.
- `explosion`: explosion anatomy, frame stepper: flash, ring, fireball, smoke, debris; play/step buttons.
- `lava`: lava crust over glowing cracks (palette cycling or scrolling noise), click to pour water: rock + steam.
- `haze`: sine row offset heat haze over a pixel scene, amplitude and frequency sliders.
