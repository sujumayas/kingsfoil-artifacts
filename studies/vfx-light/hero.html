Top-down pixel light and shadow: tiny algorithms for sun, fire, lanterns and the dark

Snow, acid, rain and trees are things in the world. Light touches every pixel of every thing: it sets the hour, marks what is safe, and gives each torch, candle and crystal a personality. Each part has a classic answer a few lines long: a light byte per tile, a palette table per level, a 10 Hz flicker string, height × cot(elevation) for sun shadows, a polar depth row for torch shadows. Aimed at Godot 4 on the web.
