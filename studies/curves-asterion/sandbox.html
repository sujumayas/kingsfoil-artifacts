Asterion curve lab

A model of Asterion's progression constants at main 8836106a (PR #183, 2026-09-11) and of the exp/h ladder its balance harness reports. It is not the game. Nothing on this page is measured in play: the server's telemetry is off, so every line is the code's formulas run against the harness's kill rates.

Model. Hours are hunting hours at the exp/h ladder below (harness -levels, 60 trials at 8 levels, median safe creature of the player's band, divided by OVERHEAD). Death cost is exp lost at the bottom of the level divided by that level's exp/h. Skill and magic assume every hour is spent at the swing rate and mana rate set in the knobs. No value here is measured.
