Asterion is a Tibia-like MMORPG built for five friends. Levels follow Tibia's cubic, sword and shield rise by use, magic level grows with mana spent, and a death costs 10 % of your exp plus a walk back to the corpse. This study prices every one of those curves in hours of play from the game's own constants and balance harness, reads them next to Tibia, Ultima Online, EverQuest, RuneScape and the games that softened death later, and turns the result into a Curve Spec, a playtest protocol for five players, a curve lab and an Atlas of every creature, item and spell.
