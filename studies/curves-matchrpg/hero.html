MatchRPG is a sliding match-3 descent to 6666 m: five worlds, an elite every 12 encounters, a world boss every 36, and a dark edge that ends the run if the board is too slow. This study reads 859 real runs from 21 players next to games built under the same conditions, from Tetris and 10000000 to Balatro and Candy Crush. It finds where the curve walls players in, what they learn between runs and what the hidden mastery clock does, then turns it into a Curve Spec, a playtest protocol and a ranked backlog.
