MatchRPG curve lab

A model of the Curve Spec's tuning constants at main 82b7eb2 (v0.7.14), with measured story and endless runs laid over it. It is not the game. Hero power comes from the run data, not from a simulation of talents, relics, items and the town.

Every number on this page is a model output unless it names its sample. Measured data covers v0.6 and v0.7, 23 October to 10 November 2026, aggregates only.

Scroll speed over a run, per tier

Model. Speed in px per real second, which is the code's px per dungeon second times the tier clock. The ramp runs only about 54% of real time, because elite fights, boss arenas and held timers stop it. That share is fitted to the measured scroll at each boss on gold and diamond. Bars show how long story runs last: 590 story runs not resumed, 18 players, grouped by depth band and placed at the band's median length.

Scroll's share of its BASE to MAX range when each world boss fight starts. Small grey numbers are measured medians from story elite and boss fights (gold 8 players, diamond 8, silver 11). Tiers differ because slower boards spend more dungeon time per wall: the model divides each tier's clock by its measured pace (bronze 0.305, silver 0.353, gold 0.443, diamond 0.506 moves per second).

The first walls, attempt by attempt

Ogre King, your knobsOgre King, currentOld Mossback, your knobsOld Mossback, currentHalf cleared

Model. Share of players who have cleared the wall by each attempt. The wound cuts the wall's HP by WOUND_STEP per loss up to WOUND_MAX, until it is beaten once. The model assumes the win chance per attempt scales with the square of the HP cut. It is calibrated so the current build gives the measured median attempts to a first clear: 3 for the Ogre King (15 players) and 7 for Old Mossback (10 players). Test any value with dev/balance_sim.gd before trusting it.

Watch: Old Mossback against the Warden. Mossback should not cost more attempts than the Warden, whose measured median is 3 (10 players). Today it costs 7.

Levels by run for a player stuck at a wall

Model. Hero level after each run for a player whose every run ends at the same world 1 wall. 72% of normal slots hold an enemy and the rest are traps. A lost wall fight pays LOST_FIGHT_XP of its XP times half its HP taken off on average. XP past a run's first level counts XP_PAST_LEVEL. Grey lines are the talent rows at levels 3, 8, 14, 21 and 29.

Watch: moving LOST_FIGHT_XP from 0.5 to 1.0 barely moves these lines. XP does not fix a wall; the wall's own numbers do.

Normal enemy power is model: HP relative to encounter 1, including each world's base stats and the lap 1 lift in worlds 1 to 3. Boss power multiplies a world boss's story HP. Log scale. Bars and the p25, median, p75 and p90 marks are measured: 192 endless runs, 11 players, at 37 m per encounter.

Watch: if the lap 1 lift puts the death peak before lap 2, the lift is too high. Today the 38 endless runs that end before 2,000 m end by retreat 92% of the time, not by death.
