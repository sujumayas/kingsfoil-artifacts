Top-down pixel magic: tiny algorithms for spell projectiles, auras, hit sparks and readable spell feedback

A spell is a few bytes and a clock. Tibia sends an effect as an id and a tile, and the client plays a flipbook at 75 ms per frame. Its missiles pick one of 9 pre-drawn frames and fly for 150 x sqrt(tiles) ms. Pokémon Emerald draws 355 moves from 192 small sprite sheets and a 48-command script. This study collects those rules, adds trails, chain lightning, hit sparks, auras, cast circles and element ramps, and measures what each one costs. A fight of 30 casters fits in 1.5 ms on our phone proxy, if the draw path is right and a clarity manager decides whose spells you see in full. Aimed at Godot 4 on the web and a server-authoritative MMO.
