Top-down pixel water: tiny algorithms for shorelines, rivers, lakes and sea, and water you can wade through

Classic water is a shared clock, not a simulation. Pokémon Red moves its whole sea by rotating the bits of one 8×8 tile, and Tibia sends no water state at all because every client runs the same 500 ms clock. This study collects the small answers for coasts, rivers, depth and the things that touch water: one distance byte per tile, a direction byte per river tile, a palette, rings, a V-shaped wake and a clip line. Aimed at Godot 4 on the web and a server-authoritative MMO.
