# Figure plan for vfx-water (shared by two figure agents)

Figure names are FIXED. Each `<!--FIG name-->…<!--/FIG-->` block lives in the file listed, its demo is registered with `reg('name', …)` in the matching demos file. Markup follows studies/vfx-fire/figs*.html exactly (`<figure class="fig" data-demo="name" id="fig-name">`, `.fig-h` with `Fig. @N`, title, `.illu` label when numbers are illustrative, canvases, controls, figcaption). Figures use only facts in studies/vfx-water/report.md; label illustrative numbers. No images, no game art: original abstract pixel drawings in code. Animations via the core.js demo runner (requestAnimationFrame, paused offscreen). Every figure must work at 390 px phone width without horizontal overflow and with zero console errors.

The recipe is studies/vfx-fire/build.py (the newest, same report layout). The scaffolded studies/vfx-water/build.py was cloned from vfx-light and is stale: Agent R rebuilds it from the fire recipe.

## Agent R (recipe owner): build.py, hero.html, core.js, style.css, study.css, figs1.html + demos1.js, figs3.html + demos3.js
figs1.html / demos1.js
- `hero` (in hero.html; demo in demos1.js): live small coast scene (sea with depth bands, foam, waves running up a beach, a river mouth); buttons "Coast", "River", "Night", "Wade".
- `timeline`: the §3 generations, clickable.
- `palcycle` (A1): palette cycling water: 3-4 reserved slots rotate at a slider rate; the tile pixels never change; a palette strip with the moving slots highlighted.
- `tileclock` (A2, 6a): animated tile frames on a shared clock vs per-tile phase; sync toggle shows the shimmer anti-pattern; frame hold slider; the §6a table timings as presets (ALttP 3x9, LA DX 4x16, Emerald 8x16, Stardew 10x200 ms).
- `hdma` (A3): row-offset sine wobble over a pixel scene, amplitude/period/speed sliders.
- `tibiapattern` (A4): Tibia-style 500 ms phase plus position pattern (big water texture repeats by world position), toggle position pattern on/off.
- `dualgrid` (B1): paint land/water on a grid by click; show blob-47 vs Wang-16 vs dual grid case indices and tile counts.
- `overlayshore` (B2): animated base water with static shore overlays on top; toggle overlays.
- `distfield` (B3): click to paint coast; multi-source BFS animates outward step by step; chamfer vs BFS toggle; colour by distance.
- `foamwaves` (B4): beach cross-section/top view: wave line runs up, foam where it breaks, retreats, wet-sand band dries; period slider.
- `tide` (B5): tide level slider/auto over a height map; shoreline moves; bytes sent counter (1 B per change).
- `flowmap` (C1): two-phase flow blending: single-phase (stretch/reset visible) vs two-phase with triangle weights; draw flow vectors by dragging.
figs3.html / demos3.js
- `score`: the §5 scorecard, sortable, with a radar for the selected row (copy vfx-fire's score figure pattern).
- `budget`: the §6c performance budget as a log-scale bar chart with a 16.7 ms frame line (label estimates as such).
- `stack` is generated by build.py from §7 (9 layers).

## Agent F: figs2.html + demos2.js only (never edit build.py, core.js, hero.html, figs1/3, demos1/3, style.css, study.css)
- `currents` (C2): Pokémon Emerald forced current tiles: walker pushed 16 px per 6 frames vs surf 8 vs walk 16, stops at collision; click to place rocks.
- `waterfall` (C3): vertical scrolling strip, lip highlight, splash and mist particles at the base; speed slider.
- `flowca` (C4): Minecraft-style levels 0-7 flowing water automaton on a small grid; click to place source/wall; tick rate slider.
- `depthramp` (D1): depth bands with hue-shifted ramp vs plain darkening; sea / river / swamp / biome tint presets; Bayer dither at band edges toggle.
- `noisebands` (D2): scrolling noise cut into a few colour bands plus thresholded highlight; band count and speed sliders.
- `caustics` (D3): cheap animated cell/Voronoi caustics on a shallow floor plus hashed glints.
- `reflect` (D4): a tree and a character over water with a flipped, darkened, row-wobbled copy clipped to water pixels; wobble amplitude slider.
- `rings` (E1): fixed ring pool (squashed ellipses for 3/4 view); click to drop; pool size display; Stardew timing (8 frames at 150 ms) as a preset.
- `kelvin` (E2): drag/auto-move a boat or swimmer; wake from spawn spacing with 19.47 degree arms; speed slider; shows the angle lines.
- `wading` (E3): walker moving from sand to shallow to deep: knee clip, ring per step, swim (head only), blocked; speed multipliers shown.
- `splash` (E4): drop objects: splash frames, rings, item sinks (trashholder) vs log floats.
- `bobbing` (E5): floating log and boat bobbing (sine amplitude/period sliders) and drifting along a current.
- `fishing` (E6/F3): bobber, nibble dips, bite with ring, click window (Stardew 800 ms); Tibia chance formula slider for skill 10-100 showing 10-50% clamp; fished-out tile regrow timer (1200 s, compressed).
- `reactions` (F4): water tile reactions: fire -> steam, frost -> ice, lightning -> electrified water, oil on water; click elements onto a grid.
- `heightfield` (G1): Hugo Elias ripples at lake scale on a coarse grid vs ring pool side by side; damping slider; cost readout.
- `waveparticles` (G2): wave particles: expanding particle fronts from a moving source, cost grows with sources not area; particle count readout.
