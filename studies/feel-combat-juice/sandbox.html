Simulation

1 · Server combat truth

The server owns cooldowns, damage and deaths. It wakes on a tick (50, 100 or 600 ms) and resolves everything due since the last one. Targeted hits resolve at cast end and cannot miss. Aimed hits are checked against server positions. Lag compensation rewinds targets by your latency plus half a tick, which favours the shooter. With it off, the server checks where targets are now, which favours the target. Ground circles resolve when the marker ends, and the hitbox matches the decal.

2 · Input, targeting and queue

Tab picks the nearest enemy in view by Chebyshev tiles, breaks ties by the smallest clockwise angle from your facing, then by unit id. In combat it only cycles enemies already fighting you, and in PvP players come before mobs. Taps and free aim use an auto pick that keeps its target unless another is a tile closer. Soft lock may bend an aimed skill's start direction by at most 15° in PvE and 0 in PvP, and clamps circles to range. The global cooldown is 1.0 s and the queue window 400 ms. One press is queued and the last one wins; it fires the moment the cooldown ends. The strip above the hotbar shows presses, fires and drops.

3 · Prediction and reconciliation

Wait for server starts nothing until the server answers, so every swing lags by a round trip plus up to one tick. Animation starts the swing at once and plays impact on confirm. Animation + effects also predicts sparks and sound. Numbers wait for the server unless you predict them. A predicted hit that the server rejects turns into a grey struck-out phantom number.

4 · Animation

A sword swing has anticipation (100 ms, weapon drawn back, 1 px lean), a strike of three 50 ms frames with the smear arc, and 150 ms of recovery, which moving cancels. You walk at half speed while swinging. Body sprites have 4 directions; the weapon and smear rotate freely. A 150 ms flinch plays only on crits or when one hit takes at least 1/16 of max life. Deaths flash for 2 frames, squash to 1.2 by 0.8 while fading, and leave a corpse at 133 ms, or swap to a corpse at once as in Tibia.

5 · Impact feedback

Hitstop freezes only sprites, and only when you are the attacker or the target: 3 frames on a hit, 5 on a heavy hit, 6 on a crit, 8 on a kill. Other players' hits get none, and PvP victims get none. It never freezes input or the server. Switch to Global to see why a whole-screen freeze breaks a shared screen. The flash is 2 full white frames plus a 4-frame fade, and keeps the dark outline so the shape reads. Normal hits recoil 2 px, crits 4 px, 3 frames out and 3 back. Cleave, Quake, the warden's slam and enemy orbs shove the target 1 tile over 150 ms on the server, snapped to the tile; a shoved player is immune to the next shove for 10 s.

6 · Camera

Shake is trauma² times 3 px, half that vertically, in whole pixels with no rotation. Trauma decays by 1.5 per second and caps at 1.0. A hit you take adds 0.2 plus 0.3 times damage over max life. Your crit adds 0.3 and kicks the camera 1 px against your aim. The warden's slam adds 0.6 at its centre, fading to nothing over 6 tiles, so slams far away barely move your view. Shake intensity goes down to 0.

7 · Combat text and telegraphs

Numbers use the bitmap atlas, one blit per glyph. They live 1 s, rise 24 px and fade after 833 ms. Same-tier numbers on one target merge for 400 ms (yours, taken, others'); crits never merge, last 1.2 s and pop to double size for 3 frames. A new number whose predecessor is under 250 ms old starts lower. At most 4 per target and 64 on screen. Other players' numbers are dimmed. Telegraph leads: trash 500 ms, caster circle and line 1.2 s, warden slam 2.5 s. The lance flies 12 tiles/s and the enemy orb 6, both 0.75 tile wide; bolts take 150 × √tiles ms. A whiff shows only after a projectile passed within half a tile.

8 · Audio

Hits are synthesized with WebAudio: a falling thump plus a noise click, a ring on crits, pitch varied by 5%. Each sound carries a Tibia-style tag (own, others, creatures, global). The pool holds 16 voices, at most 3 per sound and 40 ms between starts of one sound. A full pool steals the oldest lower-priority voice, and own hits outrank enemy telegraphs, which outrank allies. Sound starts on your first key or tap.

9 · Crowd budget, PvP and access

Other players' effects run at full, reduced (small sparks, a third of the sounds) or minimal (no sparks, allies drawn at 60%). Allies spread in a loose ring around their targets. Caps hold particles at 500 and numbers at 64, evicting other players' first. PvP halves damage, gives the victim no hitstop and no soft lock, and stuns run 100%, 50%, then immune, resetting 20 s after the last, capped at 6 s. Reduce flashing keeps big flashes under 3 per second.
