Top-down pixel fog: tiny algorithms for ground mist, cloud shadows, fog of war and atmospheric haze

Good fog is a colour problem with a tiny cost. Pokémon Emerald draws it with 20 world-locked sprites and one blend register, and Tibia skips fog of war because the server never sends what you cannot see. This study collects the small answers: a density byte per cell, a palette table that steps colours toward the sky, a height line that fills the valleys, a count per tile for fog of war. We measured what each one costs. At 320×180 almost every fog shader costs microseconds, so the real budget goes to the CPU. Aimed at Godot 4 on the web and a server-authoritative MMO.
