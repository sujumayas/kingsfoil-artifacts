Top-down pixel rain: tiny algorithms for drizzle, downpours and everything that gets wet

Any single raindrop matters for a fraction of a second. What players remember is the response: darker stone, puddles in the ruts, rings on the pond, a dry strip under the eaves, a campfire hissing out, and eaves still dripping after the sun returns. One intensity knob, one wetness byte, one water layer and one palette do most of it. Aimed at Godot 4 on the web.
