Top-down pixel fire at 32 px: scaling the fire engine to 32x32 tiles and 24x32 characters

VFX #6 built a fire engine on 8 px cells at 320x180, with flames a few pixels tall. Asterion Online has 32 px tiles and 24x32 characters, so the canvas grows to 640x360 and holds 4 times the pixels. We measured every fire layer at that size. The whole frame grows 3.0 to 3.9 times, to 0.624 ms on the desktop and 4.90 ms under a 6x CPU throttle, and two per-pixel passes, light and smoke, are most of it. The data does not have to grow. The server keeps one fire cell per tile and rolls spread once a second, which keeps VFX #6's material table and holds every front under a walking player. The art grows instead. Flames overflow the tile as Tibia's do, the burn is drawn per pixel through a painted map, and embers and smoke move in 2x2 clusters. Aimed at Godot 4 on the web.
