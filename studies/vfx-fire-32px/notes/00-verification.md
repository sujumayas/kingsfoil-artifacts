## Checks and corrections
- Torch flame size: note 11 proposes 10x16 px for a torch (from brullov's 10x24 and a 32 px torch pack); note 13 proposes about 6x8 px for a hand-held torch (a quarter of the character height). Lead decision: 10x16 for torches on posts and walls, about 6x8 for a torch carried by a 24x32 character. Both are inferences from packs, not from a shipped game's data.
- Tibia draws no burning overlay on creatures and no carried torch (note 13, from TFS and OTClient). Any overlay or drawn torch in the report is an Asterion addition and must be labelled [I].
- Tibia areas are built from 1-tile effects (note 14): a great fireball is 37 tiles of a 32x32 effect, not one large sprite. The 64x64 explosion in note 11 is a pack reference for non-Tibia styles.
- Correction to VFX #6: the Stardew floor torch flame is 8x8 px, 7 frames x 100 ms (1.5.4 `Torch.cs`); the 12x11 4-frame flame VFX #6 called "the torch" is the campfire, fireplace and torch-furniture flame (note 10). The report's §12 lists this.
- Correction to VFX #6: Tibia fire field stage times are 200 / 148 / 98 s in Canary's data, against TFS 1.4's 3 x 120 s quoted in VFX #6. Both are server data from different forks; say which.
- Note 10's Tibia sprite numbers come from decoding Canary's `appearances.dat` with a script in `scratch-10/decode_app.py`. The 64x64 canvas and bottom-right anchoring are inferred from box coordinates (x + w = 64) and OTClient `thingtype.cpp`; mark the overflow as [I] built on [V] boxes.
- Note 12 Q5 says VFX #6 measured smoke advect plus decay at 0.31 ms "on an 80x45 field". Wrong: VFX #6 §6d gives 0.31 ms for a 320x180 field (57,600 cells). My CONTEXT line was ambiguous. So 8 px cells at 640x360 (3,600 cells) is far below 0.31 ms. Use note 16's measured numbers.
- Correction to VFX #6: its "flames reach about 72 rows" is the double-buffered height. Sanglard's exact in-place loop reaches a median 95 rows at L 36 (note 12, measured over its own simulations). VFX #6 §1 item 1 should be listed in §12.
- Spread at 32 px: note 15 measured that VFX #6's per-tick table on 32 px tiles is 4x faster in px/s; the decision is note 15's "Option A" (1 Hz rolls per tile, same table). Relayed to the sandbox builder.
- Note 18 (fact-check) confirmed: OTClient effect and missile constants, the light falloff, the damage text constants, Stardew's 8x8 7 x 100 ms floor torch, Canary's 200 / 148 / 98 s, 25.3% lone pixels (re-run 25.5%), front speeds 0.52 and 0.93 cells per tick, the Godot claims and RimWorld's edge-ring rule.
- Note 18 wording fixes: the DOOM heights 95 vs 73 rows are "the row where half the columns are lit" (re-run 93 vs 72); the median highest lit pixel per column gives 105 vs 78. The report names the measure. Mehah `waitFor` refuses at `<=` the minimum age, which is the idle animator's value when the dat has one, else 75 ms. 5 x 120 ms is fire field stage 1 only; stages 2 and 3 are 5 x 100 ms. Don't Starve's 10 / 20 / 30 s burn presets come from `standardcomponents.lua`; `tuning.lua` gives 7.5 / 15 / 30 s.
- Effect timing: note 14's flat 75 ms per frame is edubart OTClient's constant (confirmed by note 18). Modern Tibia data stores per-frame times in appearances.dat (note 17: FIREAREA 40 ms + 13 x 60 ms = 820 ms; EXPLOSIONHIT 35 to 110 ms easing, 660 ms). Both are right for their client; the report says the 75 ms rule is the old fixed-rate client and the protobuf data gives per-frame times. Note 17 maps client ids to `const.h` names by inference.
- Sandbox first pass (13 Nov), lead's screenshot review: fire shadows drawn as large person-shaped silhouettes (spec says a 16x10 ellipse), spread speed 0.26 / 0.32 / 0.33 tiles/s at 32 / 16 / 8 px (should match, and note 15 measured 0.52 at 32 px), wind +4% at strength 5 (Alexandridis gives x1.25 downwind at 5 m/s), faint smoke, a stamped ash pattern, a showcase that is mostly burnt ground. Sent back for a polish pass. The sandbox departs from the spec on purpose in three places: tree 24 s and wood wall 30 s burn times, no 64x64 explosion (tiled areas only), field stages at Canary's times divided by 10.
- Lead check of §7 pseudocode (13 Nov): the server tick (hash precedence, integer chances, IGNITING flip, roll phase from the ignition tick), the entry-direction threshold (sign and diagonal scaling), the DOOM loop (R6 weights, sideways write, padding, the /9 shift) and the rim shader read correctly. One fix: the torch comment gave L = 8 for a 16 px flame, which is the double-buffered value; the in-place loop shown needs L = 6 (16 / 2.6). Corrected in report.md.
- Figure agents' §7 ports (13 Nov) found 20 report problems, sent to the writer in one batch. The real bugs: the server tick's entry direction depended on iteration order, `budget_ok` never consumed budget, rain at 20% per 4 Hz tick (59% a second), the claim that roll load spreads over four ticks (one fire rolls on one tick of four), burn bands 1.4 to 2x wider on diagonal entries, a TONE table whose white core never shows after smoothing, a rim shader that lands on the wrong side of flipped sprites, and no damage period for burning characters (a 4 Hz flash breaks the 3-per-second flash limit).
- The continuous arrival-time burn front (note 12 Q4) is the researcher's own construction. The report labels it [I] and the sandbox shows it beside per-tile progress.
