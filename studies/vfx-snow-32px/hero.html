Top-down pixel snow at 32 px: scaling the snow engine to 32x32 tiles and 24x32 characters

VFX #1 stored one byte of snow depth per pixel and drew snow where the depth passed a dither threshold, on 8 px tiles. Asterion Online has 32 px tiles and 24x32 characters, so the canvas grows to 640x360 and holds 4 times the pixels. A straight port costs 4.0 to 4.3 times the old frame and fills 87 to 93% of a 16.7 ms frame on our phone proxy. This study keeps the field at 320x180, samples it bilinearly, swaps the Bayer matrix for a painted threshold map and draws the ground in one shader. The frame then costs 0.47 to 0.56 ms on the same proxy. It also resizes what the artist sees, with boot prints on the walk cycle's contact frames, a 6 px sink and flakes of 1, 2 and 3 px. Aimed at Godot 4 on the web.
