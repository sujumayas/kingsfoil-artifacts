@@TITLE@@

Hearthguard is a co-op hero defense: one to three heroes, five chapters of six waves, and a Hearth that ends the run when it falls. This study looks at games built under the same conditions, from Warcraft III custom maps to Kingdom Rush, Left 4 Dead and Deep Rock Galactic. It traces which curve patterns made them fun, checks what happened when their designers changed them, and turns the result into a small Curve Spec, a playtest protocol for five players and a ranked backlog.
