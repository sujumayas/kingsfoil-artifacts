# 03. Twins, lineage B: the modern twins

Researcher notes for the Hearthguard Curves study. Angle: the post-WC3 games that took the custom-map patterns
(hero defense, lanes, wave sets, co-op player scaling) and refined them. WC3 maps themselves are in a sibling file.
Already-covered material is cited from the glossary notes, not re-researched (L4D director, RoR2 formula).

Access notes. Every fandom.com wiki returned HTTP 402 (Kingdom Rush, Orcs Must Die, Vampire Survivors). I used
wiki.gg mirrors (vampire.survivors.wiki, deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg, helldivers.wiki.gg) and wikily.gg for Kingdom Rush.
GameSpot returned 403. Where only a search snippet was available the claim is tagged lower.

## Q1. Which modern twins share Hearthguard's conditions?

**Takeaway.** No single modern game matches all six conditions. The closest are co-op games with fixed content and a
short run (Deep Rock Galactic, Aghanim's Labyrinth, Orcs Must Die) and a solo browser-mobile game with fixed waves and
selectable modes (Kingdom Rush). Vampire Survivors and Risk of Rain match the run shape and power curve but not the
randomness. Diablo matters for one formula, co-op HP scaling.

### Twin table

Condition key: ● same as Hearthguard, ◐ partly, ○ different. Hearthguard: real-time mouse/touch one hero; one run of
15-40 min; low randomness; high cost of failure (Hearth falls = restart); solo or co-op 1-3, lane each; browser and phone.

### Cited findings
- Kingdom Rush 1 HP multiplier 0.8 / 1.0 / 1.2 for Casual / Normal / Veteran, enemy speed unchanged, and player soldiers
  and heroes get 120% HP on Casual. [V, E3] [S2]
- Kingdom Rush 6 Genesis has four modes. Single-value enemies scale to 70 / 80 / 100 / 110% HP, rounded up to the next 10.
  Impossible adds 110% enemy speed and 150% hero XP and unlocks after winning stage 18. Casual pays 120% enemy gold.
  Hand-tuned enemies vary more: Gor'Thok 5,600 / 7,200 / 8,000 / 13,000. [V, E3] [S1]
- Impossible first appeared in Kingdom Rush: Vengeance (2018). [U, E3] [S4] (fandom blocked, snippet only)
- An Ironhide founder said the first game was balanced "normal and more casual" and Veteran was added afterwards. [U, E2] [S5] (Spanish snippet)
- Heroic Challenge is 1 life and 6 waves; Iron Challenge is 1 life and 1 wave (KR6) with tower limits. Each win gives 1 star
  in the first game; Vengeance and Alliance give no upgrade points for them. [V, E3] [S3]
- Diablo 2 1.10 and later multiply monster HP and XP by `(n+1)/2` for n players. Before 1.09 HP scaled `X × n`.
  Minions stopped scaling in 1.10. [V, E3] [S15]
- Dungeon Defenders II fixed multiplayer enemy HP scaling that was "higher than intended", from +50% to +25% per player. [U, E2] [S9] (changelog snippet)
- Deep Rock Galactic tables are in Q3. [V, E3] [S10]
- Vampire Survivors stage caps are 15, 20 or 30 minutes. At the cap the Reaper spawns with 65,535 power (one hit ends
  the run), and one more spawns each minute. Surviving the cap counts as a clear and pays 500 gold. [V, E3] [S12]
- Aghanim's Labyrinth tiers are Apprentice, Magician, Sorcerer, Grand Magus, and Apex Mage was added later. The same update
  raised encounter buffs from 3 to 4. [V, E3] [S22] Valve's page still says four Ascension levels. [V, E2] [S23]
- Helldivers 2 has 10 difficulties. XP and Requisition bonus is 0% at 1-2, then 25, 50, 75, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300%.
  New threats arrive by tier (rare samples at 4, operation modifiers at 5 and 8, super samples at 6). [V, E3] [S21]
  Higher tiers unlock by completing a mission on your current highest. [U, E3] [S21b] (guide snippet)

### Inferences
- [I] The twins split into two families. Fixed-content games (Kingdom Rush, OMD2, Dungeon Defenders, DRG, Helldivers) tune
  difficulty with selectable bands. Random-content runs (VS, RoR, Aghanim) tune it with a clock or player-steered picks.
  Hearthguard is fixed content, so the first family's tools fit without change.
- [I] Every co-op twin with published numbers scales enemies sublinearly with players. D2 goes 1.0, 1.5, 2.0 for 1-3.
  DDII landed at +25% per player. Hearthguard gives boss HP 2.5x for three [K, E1] [01-current-build.md:98], which
  is above all of them. That may be right because each player holds a separate lane, but it should be measured.

### Gaps
- No published per-player numbers for OMD2, Aghanim's Labyrinth or Siltbreaker. Robot's "Solo and Multiplayer Balancing"
  post for Deathtrap exists but was not read.
- No Kingdom Rush lives-per-star thresholds (fandom blocked). Commonly stated as 3 stars at 18+ of 20 lives; not verified.
- Sanctum: no difficulty data found in budget. Gauntlet Legends: no scaling data.

## Q2. How did the modern twins change older patterns, and why?

**Takeaway.** Four refinements recur. Co-op scaling moved from linear to sublinear. Grades moved from a binary 5-skull or
3-star cap toward scores and opt-in ladders. Endless modes became fixed-length challenge modes. Checkpoint-free co-op
campaigns got patched down after launch backlash.

### Cited findings (natural experiments)

| # | Game | When | Change | Reaction | Grade | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Diablo 2 | Patch 1.10, 2003 | Monster HP per player from `X × n` to `X × (n+1)/2` | Became the standard; D2R kept it | E3 [S15] | Linear per-player HP makes co-op slower than solo |
| 2 | Dungeon Defenders II | 2014 (early access) | Per-player HP scaling +50% cut to +25%, called a bug | Patch note only | E2 [S9] [U] | Even +50% per player was too much in a tower-plus-hero game |
| 3 | Dota 2 Siltbreaker | June 2017 | Launch with no checkpoint restart; NPC death in escorts is an instant fail | Kotaku: a wipe can cost "upwards of an hour"; Valve patched difficulty (Midas removed, gold cap 5,000 to 5,500, star timers loosened) | E3 [S24] [S25] | Long co-op attempts plus instant fails turn hard into unfair |
| 4 | Kingdom Rush series | 2011 to 2018 | Veteran added after a casual-first balance; Impossible added in Vengeance and gated | Series kept four modes through KR6 | E2/E3 [S5] [S4] [S1] | Ship one tuned middle mode, add harder bands once you know the ceiling |
| 5 | Vampire Survivors | July 2022 hotfix | Killing the Reaper before 31:00 counts as surviving 31 min | Wiki records it | E3 [S12] | The cap is a finish line, not a punishment; reward reaching it |
| 6 | Back 4 Blood | Oct to Dec 2021 | Studio said spawns were higher than intended (duplicated spawn cards); December patch aimed to "decrease the disparity between the various difficulties" | Players demanded devs play Nightmare; mixed reaction to the Nov patch | E2 [S19] [S20] | Bugs in a spawn director read as bad difficulty; tier gaps should be even |
| 7 | Orcs Must Die! Deathtrap | Aug 2024 | Dropped the 5-skull grade and par time; scores run to dozens or hundreds | Designers: it "became binary", five skulls or fail | E2 [S6] | A capped grade stops giving information once most players cap it |
| 8 | Orcs Must Die! Deathtrap | Sep 2025 | Endurance: 25 waves, starts at difficulty 10, scales HP and damage every 7th wave; Nightmare Endurance at difficulty 20 | Designers: first 10 Endless waves are "rote and easy"; default coins were "easy to an unfun degree" | E2 [S7] | Skip rote waves; a fixed end removes the need for runaway scaling |
| 9 | Diablo 4 | Vessel of Hatred, Oct 2024 | World Tiers 1-4 removed; Normal, Hard, Expert, Penitent always open; Torment 1-4 gated at level 60 | Press frames it as answering player drop-off | E3 [S17] [S18] | Don't gate the easy bands; gate only the top |
| 10 | Aghanim's Labyrinth | 2020 update | Apex Mage tier added; encounter buffs 3 to 4; new modifiers | No numbers on reception found | E3 [S22] | Ladders grow at the top after players clear the old top |

### Inferences
- [I] The WC3 custom-map habit was a vote at game start ("-hard", "-insane") with flat multipliers. The modern twins kept
  the up-front choice (difficulty mode) but added two things: a reward multiplier per tier (DRG +25% to +133%, Helldivers
  0% to +300%) and an unlock gate on the top tiers only. The gate keeps the top tier meaningful without blocking friends.
- [I] OMD's move from skulls to scores and KR's move to Heroic/Iron challenges answer the same problem. Once a player
  clears a stage at the cap, the grade has nothing left to say. KR answers with a new variant of the same stage; OMD with
  an unbounded score.
- [I] Siltbreaker is the closest MOBA-control twin to Hearthguard and its main lesson is negative. Combine a long attempt
  with a full-wipe reset and the cost of failure outruns what four friends will tolerate. Hearthguard's run is 24-27 min
  [K, E1] with a full restart on loss, which is near the edge Siltbreaker crossed.

### Gaps
- Siltbreaker patch numbers come from one Russian-language snippet [S25]. The "Siltbreaker Hard Mode" Steam changelog
  (reward 60/80/100% plus 15% per level) looks like a community workshop map, not Valve. [U]
- Vermintide 2's April 2018 difficulty patch could not be read (GameSpot 403). No before/after numbers.
- No Blizzard verbatim quote on why World Tiers went away.

## Q3. What do the co-op twins do for 1-3 players?

**Takeaway.** Deep Rock Galactic publishes the best table in the set. It scales three things separately by player count:
how many enemies (most), how tough big enemies are (some), and how hard they hit (a little). Solo gets a spawn floor equal
to two players "to compensate for the loss of Bosco", the solo drone. Hearthguard has no AI teammate, so its solo floor
is the lane itself.

### Cited findings
DRG Hazard 3 and Hazard 5, solo / 2P / 3P / 4P. [V, E3] [S10]

| Hazard | Enemy count | Normal HP | Large HP | Elite HP | Enemy damage | Hazard bonus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.15 / 0.25 / 0.45 / 0.65 | 0.70 | 0.45 / 0.55 / 0.70 / 0.85 | 0.60 / 0.75 / 0.85 / 1.10 | 0.50 flat | +25% |
| 3 | 0.50 / 0.50 / 0.80 / 1.10 | 1.10 | 0.80 / 0.90 / 1.00 / 1.10 | 0.90 / 1.10 / 1.30 / 1.30 | 1.20 / 1.30 / 1.40 / 1.50 | +75% |
| 5 | 0.85 / 0.85 / 1.25 / 1.50 | 1.20 | 1.20 / 1.20 / 1.40 / 1.50 | 1.30 / 1.30 / 1.50 / 1.65 | 2.80 / 3.00 / 3.20 / 3.40 | +133% |

- Revive health falls from 0.60 at Hazard 1 to 0.10 at Hazard 5; regen cap from 15% to 10% at Hazard 5. [V, E3] [S10]
- Hazard 5+ adds four optional modifiers in two levels each (Aggressive, More Enemies x1.2/x1.5, Player Vulnerability
  x1.5/x2, Tough Enemies). [V, E3] [S10]
- Normal enemies never scale HP with player count; only Large, XL and Elite do. [V, E3] [S10]

### Inferences
- [I] At Hazard 3, going from 1 to 3 players multiplies enemy count by 1.6, large-enemy HP by 1.25 and damage by 1.17.
  Total threat grows by about 2.3x for 3x the players. That matches D2's `(n+1)/2` = 2.0x for three players in spirit.
- [I] The split matters for Hearthguard because each player holds a lane. More players should mean more lanes are live,
  not each lane being tougher. Scaling the shared threats (boss, Hearth damage) is where co-op scaling belongs.
- [I] "Normal mobs never scale with player count" is a cheap rule that keeps the mow feeling in co-op.

### Gaps
- DRG table has no version or patch number; last wiki edit 2 October 2025.
- Helldivers 2 player-count scaling not documented on the wiki.

## Pattern cards

### M1. Sublinear co-op scaling

*Lineage.* Diablo 2 linear `X × n` (2000) → D2 1.10 `X × (n+1)/2` (2003) → Dungeon Defenders II +50% cut to +25% per
player (2014) → Deep Rock Galactic per-axis tables (2020). Why: linear per-player HP made group play slower than solo.

*Feeling.* Fellowship. A second player makes the run feel easier and louder, not longer.

*Mechanism.*

    shared_hp(n) = base × (n + 1) / 2        # n = 1, 2, 3 -> 1.0, 1.5, 2.0
    lane mobs: no per-player scaling (DRG "normal" rule)

*Works when / breaks when.* Works when players add damage roughly in proportion. Breaks when one player carries; then
the scaled boss is too much for the others' lanes.

*Evidence.* [V, E3, S15] D2 formula. [U, E2, S9] DDII cut. [V, E3, S10] DRG normal enemies never scale HP.

*Cost and knobs.* S. Knob: per-player step (0.5 in D2, 0.25 in DDII), which targets scale (boss, Hearth damage, elites).

*In Hearthguard.* Boss HP is 2.5x for three players and the Hearth 2x [K, E1]. D2's rule gives 2.0x. Log boss
time-to-kill by party size before changing it; with lanes, 2.5x may be fine.

*Ratings 1–5 (author judgement):* Fun impact 3 · Evidence 4 · Cheapness 5 · Fit 4 · Testability 4

### M2. Count first, toughness second, damage last

*Lineage.* L4D director adds threats, not stats (glossary 00b [S1]) → DRG per-player tables (2020). Why: more bodies
reads as "more players, more fun"; tougher bodies read as a slog.

*Feeling.* Sensation and Fellowship. Bigger crowds to mow together.

*Mechanism.* From DRG Hazard 3, 1P→3P: count x1.6, large HP x1.25, damage x1.17. Order the three knobs by size.

*Works when / breaks when.* Works with area damage and a mow. Breaks on phones if the count blows the frame budget.

*Evidence.* [V, E3, S10].

*Cost and knobs.* S. Three multipliers per party size.

*In Hearthguard.* Each lane is one player, so this applies to shared beats only: the w4 Horde surges and the w6 adds.
Raise add count with party size before boss HP.

*Ratings 1–5 (author judgement):* Fun impact 3 · Evidence 3 · Cheapness 4 · Fit 3 · Testability 4

### M3. Narrow difficulty bands around one tuned mode

*Lineage.* WC3 "-hard" votes (sibling notes) → Kingdom Rush 0.8 / 1.0 / 1.2 HP (2011) → KR Vengeance adds Impossible
(2018) → KR6 70 / 80 / 100 / 110% plus speed and XP (2025). Why: one hand-tuned middle mode; easier and harder bands
are cheap multipliers on it.

*Feeling.* Autonomy (SDT) at the start; Challenge for those who ask for it.

*Mechanism.* Casual: mob HP x0.8, hero HP x1.2, gold x1.2. Normal: x1.0. Hard: mob HP x1.2. Top: mob HP x1.1-1.6 hand-tuned
plus speed x1.1, gated behind a clear.

*Works when / breaks when.* Works with fixed waves, because the bands keep the wave design intact. Breaks if the middle
mode is not tuned first; KR shipped casual-first and added Veteran later [S5].

*Evidence.* [V, E3, S1] [V, E3, S2] [U, E2, S5].

*Cost and knobs.* S. One multiplier row per band.

*In Hearthguard.* No modes today. Real players stall at chapter 2 [K]. A Casual band (mob HP x0.8, Hearth HP x1.2) is
the cheapest test of whether chapters 3-5 are fun at all for people.

*Ratings 1–5 (author judgement):* Fun impact 4 · Evidence 4 · Cheapness 5 · Fit 5 · Testability 5

### M4. Post-clear variant of the same content

*Lineage.* Kingdom Rush Heroic (1 life, 6 waves) and Iron (1 life, 1 wave, tower limits), 2011 → OMD Deathtrap Challenge
and Endurance modes (2025). Why: once a stage is cleared at the cap, a variant reuses the map with a new constraint.

*Feeling.* Challenge and Fiero, on content the player already groks.

*Mechanism.* Unlock after a clear. Same map; change one rule (one life, one long wave, restricted kit).

*Works when / breaks when.* Works with hand-made levels and a small team. Breaks if it is the only late-game content.

*Evidence.* [V, E3, S3] [V, E2, S7].

*Cost and knobs.* M. Knobs: which rule changes, unlock condition.

*In Hearthguard.* A "Hearth at 1,000 HP" chapter challenge, or a single-chapter run of chapter 5, unlocked after a full clear.

*Ratings 1–5 (author judgement):* Fun impact 3 · Evidence 3 · Cheapness 3 · Fit 4 · Testability 3

### M5. Unbounded grade instead of a capped one

*Lineage.* OMD2 5 skulls (4 for Rift Points left, 1 for time) → Deathtrap scores (2024). Kingdom Rush stars by lives
left. Why (Robot): the 5-skull grade "became binary".

*Feeling.* Mastery and "one more try".

*Mechanism.* Score = Hearth HP left + early-call seconds + chapter time, uncapped. Keep stars only as a floor marker.

*Works when / breaks when.* Works for replay. Breaks for first-timers if the number means nothing; show a target.

*Evidence.* [V, E2, S6] [U, E3, S8].

*Cost and knobs.* S. Score weights.

*In Hearthguard.* Hearth HP left per wave is already loggable [K]. A chapter score from Hearth HP left plus Space seconds
skipped rewards the early-call habit the game already pays gold for.

*Ratings 1–5 (author judgement):* Fun impact 3 · Evidence 3 · Cheapness 5 · Fit 4 · Testability 4

### M6. Opt-in ladder with a reward multiplier, top tiers gated

*Lineage.* WC3 difficulty votes → DRG Hazard 1-5 with +25% to +133% bonus (2020) → Helldivers 2, 10 tiers, 0% to +300%,
clear-to-unlock (2024) → Diablo 4 opens the lower four, gates Torment (2024). Why: the bonus pays players to climb, the
gate keeps the top meaningful, open low tiers keep friends together.

*Feeling.* Challenge with Autonomy; Fiero at each new tier.

*Mechanism.* Tier n: threat x(1 + 0.2n), reward x(1 + 0.25n). Tier n+1 unlocks on a clear of tier n. Lower tiers always open.

*Works when / breaks when.* Works with meta rewards to multiply. Breaks without them; Hearthguard has no meta-progression yet.

*Evidence.* [V, E3, S10] [V, E3, S21] [V, E3, S17].

*Cost and knobs.* M. Tier count, threat step, reward step.

*In Hearthguard.* Later. Needs something to spend rewards on across runs. Until then, use M3 bands.

*Ratings 1–5 (author judgement):* Fun impact 3 · Evidence 4 · Cheapness 3 · Fit 2 · Testability 3

### M7. Player-steered pressure stat (Curse)

*Lineage.* RoR difficulty over time (glossary 00b [S18]) → Vampire Survivors Curse (2022). Why: let players buy power at
the price of pressure, inside the run.

*Feeling.* Expression and Challenge. The player chooses how loud the run gets.

*Mechanism.* One stat raises enemy HP, speed, spawn rate and count by its percentage. Items that give power also add it.
No cap. VS caps enemies on screen at 500.

*Works when / breaks when.* Works when the player understands the trade. Breaks if hidden; then it is rubber banding.

*Evidence.* [V, E3, S11].

*Cost and knobs.* S. Percentage per source.

*In Hearthguard.* Space early-call is already a small Curse: more pressure for 2 gold a second [K]. A talent row or Town
building that adds +10% mob count for +X% gold would extend it. This is player-steered difficulty, not DDA.

*Ratings 1–5 (author judgement):* Fun impact 4 · Evidence 3 · Cheapness 4 · Fit 4 · Testability 4

### M8. Fixed finish line

*Lineage.* Gauntlet health drain as a clock (1985) → RoR teleporter → Vampire Survivors 15/20/30-minute cap with the Reaper
(2022) → July 2022 hotfix rewarding a Reaper kill as a full clear. Why: a known end makes a run a session, not a chore.

*Feeling.* Tension that rises to a known point, then relief.

*Mechanism.* Run length is fixed and shown. The final beat is the hardest and the clear is explicit.

*Works when / breaks when.* Works for short sessions. Breaks if the end is unwinnable and unexplained.

*Evidence.* [V, E3, S12] [V, E3, S28].

*Cost and knobs.* S. Already present.

*In Hearthguard.* 5 chapters x 6 waves is a fixed finish line [K]. Show it: a run bar with 30 notches and the time so far.

*Ratings 1–5 (author judgement):* Fun impact 3 · Evidence 3 · Cheapness 5 · Fit 5 · Testability 3

### M9. Start where it gets interesting; end before it runs away

*Lineage.* WC3 endless survival maps → OMD Endless → Deathtrap Endurance (2025). Why (Robot): first waves "rote and easy";
a 25-wave end removes the need for extra scaling like CC resistance.

*Feeling.* Challenge without the warm-up grind.

*Mechanism.* Challenge modes start mid-curve (difficulty 10) and step every 7th wave. A fixed end lets the curve stay simple.

*Works when / breaks when.* Works for returning players. Breaks for new ones; keep the warm-up in the main run.

*Evidence.* [V, E2, S7].

*Cost and knobs.* S. Start chapter, step cadence.

*In Hearthguard.* A "start at chapter 3" option for players who cleared chapter 2, to test chapters 3-5 directly. It doubles
as a playtest tool, since real players never get past chapter 2 [K].

*Ratings 1–5 (author judgement):* Fun impact 4 · Evidence 3 · Cheapness 4 · Fit 5 · Testability 5

### Anti-patterns found
- Hour-long co-op attempts with full-wipe resets and instant-fail escorts (Siltbreaker, 2017). [V, E3] [S24]
- Exponential tier numbers that stop meaning anything: Diablo 3 Torment XIII at 2,108,607% HP and 28,832% damage. [V, E2] [S16]
- Spawn bugs read as tuning (Back 4 Blood, 2021). Test the director's actual spawn counts per tier. [V, E2] [S19]
- Uneven gaps between tiers (Back 4 Blood's stated December goal). [V, E2] [S20]

## Twin list: confirm, prune, extend

- Confirm: Kingdom Rush, Orcs Must Die, Deep Rock Galactic, Vampire Survivors, Risk of Rain, Diablo 2, Dota 2 events.
- Prune: Sanctum (FPS input, no data). Gauntlet Legends (no scaling data; keep 1985 Gauntlet only as clock lineage).
  Diablo 3 (only as an anti-pattern). Dota 2 Overthrow and Custom Hero Chaos (PvP, not a difficulty curve).
- Extend: Helldivers 2 (ladder and rewards), Back 4 Blood (natural experiment), Vermintide 2 (L4D lineage with tiers;
  data gap), Diablo 4 (gating experiment).

### Ranked 8 for Hearthguard

1. Kingdom Rush. Fixed waves, browser-to-mobile, and the cleanest difficulty bands and post-clear variants.
2. Deep Rock Galactic. The only published 1-4 player tables split by count, toughness and damage.
3. Orcs Must Die (2 and Deathtrap). Lane defense with a hero and a shared Hearth, plus designer statements on grades and Endurance.
4. Aghanim's Labyrinth. Same MOBA controls, co-op run, named tiers that grew at the top.
5. Diablo 2. The `(n+1)/2` rule is the simplest co-op formula with 20 years of use.
6. Vampire Survivors. The mow power curve, Curse as player-steered pressure, a fixed finish line.
7. Siltbreaker. The closest control twin; its launch is the cost-of-failure warning for a 25-minute co-op run.
8. Helldivers 2. A ladder with reward multipliers and clear-to-unlock, for when meta-progression exists.

Risk of Rain stays a lineage source for difficulty over time (already in the glossary), not a top-8 twin, because its
randomness is far from Hearthguard's.

## Glossary additions
- Party scaling: how enemy stats or counts change with the number of players. Say which axis (count, HP, damage) and
  whether it is linear or sublinear. D2 `(n+1)/2` is the reference sublinear rule.
- Post-clear variant: the same level replayed with one changed rule, unlocked by a clear (KR Heroic and Iron).
- Pressure stat: a player-raised stat that adds threat in exchange for power (VS Curse). A kind of player-steered difficulty.
