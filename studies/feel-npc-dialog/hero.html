Top-down pixel NPCs and dialog: bubbles, boxes, portraits, quest givers and the feel of talking to a world

Talking to a townsperson looks simple and hides most of an MMO's hard problems. A Tibia NPC is a chat parser with a 3-tile radius that waits 1 s before every reply on purpose, and many players can talk to it at once, each with private replies. This study collects how games make that feel good: typewriter speed and pauses, a guard against mashing, a 9-slice box with a bobbing arrow, bubbles that stay readable in a crowd, markers, shops and barks. It also covers the server side, sessions and line ids. We measured what text costs. Once bubbles are cached it is cheap, so feel, clarity and authoring decide. Aimed at Godot 4 on the web and a server-authoritative MMO.
