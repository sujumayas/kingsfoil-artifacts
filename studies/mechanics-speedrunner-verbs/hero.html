SpeedRunner verbs: dash, crawl, grapple, dive, draw and the other mechanics the great runners added, and which ones fit a samurai time attack

SpeedRunner has six verbs on one button. Run, a variable jump, a wall slide, a wall jump, a roll and a cosmetic flip. Down and Up do nothing, and the katana comes out only in the 3.7 s duel after the flag. Mechanics #1 settled the kit; this study asks what the same games added on top of it. Celeste's dash at 2.67x run for 150 ms, Super Mario 64's long jump and ground pound, Spelunky's ledge grab, SpeedRunners' hook, Jetpack Joyride's one touch, Jump King's held charge, Katana ZERO's deflect inside the slash, Neon White's cards and Ultimate Chicken Horse's placed block. It profiles 39 verbs across eight families, prices each in buttons as much as in lines, and recommends eight that need no new input. A crouch that becomes a slide at 280 u/s, a slide-jump at the hyper's 546 x 622 u/s, a stomp, an automatic mantle, a pogo, an iai dash-slash on a 200 ms hold, the deflect as a property of the slash, and a ring hook capped at 760 u/s. Every figure runs the game's constants and the verb table of §7.
