SpeedRunner mechanics: movement, animation, elements, enemies, maps, curves and the race, from the games that did it best

SpeedRunner is a single-file Canvas2D samurai time attack. It steps physics at 1/120 s, runs at 420 u/s, jumps 3.0 body lengths with a variable cut, grants 100 ms of coyote time and a 130 ms buffer, and wall jumps away or climbs. The movement already sits in the canon of Celeste, Super Mario Bros. and N++. What is missing sits around it. Every death replays a 3 s countdown, so death to control is 3.4 s where Super Meat Boy and Trackmania stay under 1 s. There is no corner correction, no speed tech, no enemies, no medals, and the ghost stores 100 KB of poses per minute where one byte of input per step would do. This study profiles 40 techniques across eight areas, from the jump arc to server re-simulation of the input log, and ranks what SpeedRunner should adopt first. Every figure is drawn in the game's ink and runs the game's constants.
