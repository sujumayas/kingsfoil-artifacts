Walk with the arrows or WASD, or tap where the traveller should go. Keys: 1-4 tools, F next fog mode, T auto time, V next view, Space pause. The day runs on a 2 minute clock and the fog field updates at 10 Hz (both illustrative). Only the weather bytes go over the wire.

Simulation

1 · Server weather and vision

The server holds a 5 byte weather state per region: fog density, cloud cover, wind x and y, and a fog ceiling. It ticks every 2 s and sends a 7 byte packet only when a byte changes. Radiation fog follows the clock: it forms at night in low, wet ground and burns off after sunrise (illustrative curve). The server also decides which creatures you may see, so a creature in the dark is never sent.

2 · Density field

An 80 by 45 grid of 4 px cells. Each cell's target is density times a Beer's law height factor, 1 - exp(-k·max(0, ceiling - height)), times moisture, so fog pools in the valley and the marsh. At 10 Hz the field is advected by the wind, blurred a little and pulled toward its target. Frames in between lerp the last two updates. The traveller and each lamp push a hole into it.

3 · Drifting texture

Two layers read one precomputed 128 by 128 tileable fbm table: a large layer at half scale and a detail layer, scrolled slowly in different directions and domain-warped by a 64 by 64 offset table. That is 4 array reads per pixel. Procedural fbm computes 5 value-noise octaves per pixel instead. Watch the HUD.

4 · Fog pass

Every pixel is a palette index plus a fog level from 0 to 5. The final colour comes from an 8192 entry table rebuilt when the time of day moves, so fog always takes the sky colour. Palette steps moves each colour 1 to 4 steps toward a pale, low-saturation fog colour, with a narrow Bayer band only at step edges. Bayer dither only picks scene or fog. Alpha blend mixes RGB per pixel and leaves the palette.

5 · Clouds and shadows

Cloud cover picks a percentile threshold on a scrolling cloud table. Shadows take one darker palette step, offset by the sun direction, and fade out at night. Overhead clouds drift 1.9 times faster. They add fog steps rather than a new colour, so they stay low contrast, and they dither away near the screen centre and over the traveller.

6 · Atmosphere

Haze tints high ground and the top of the screen toward the sky colour and lowers contrast. Lamps light dithered rings whose radius grows with the fog around them. At dawn 4 pre-drawn slanted shafts brighten the scene by one or two palette steps, with slow light bands drifting down them, using a cached pixel list. Radial blur is the expensive comparison: a bright pass blurred toward the sun with N samples per pixel.

7 · Fog of war

Recursive shadowcasting on the 40 by 23 cell grid with radius 20. It reruns only when the traveller changes cell or an opaque cell changes. Visible cells fade in over 0.3 s. Corner values are bilinearly upsampled with a Bayer threshold. Fully visible 8 by 8 blocks are skipped. Explored cells are drawn dark and desaturated, and unknown cells are solid dark.

8 · Magic fog

The fog cloud tool adds density to a second field that drifts with the wind and decays by 4.5 % per update, about 6 s. Any 8 px cell whose spell fog is over the threshold counts as opaque, so the FOV is recomputed and sight is blocked.

9 · Palette and access

Night, dawn, morning, day and dusk palettes blend smoothly. Reduce motion stops all drift, flicker and shaft pulsing while the fog keeps its shape. Fog strength thins the fog for low-vision players. The dither pattern is locked to world pixels, so it never swims.
