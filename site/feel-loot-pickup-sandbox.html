1 · Server loot truth

The server rolls the drop when a monster dies and owns every item as an entity with an id, position, stack count, owner and timers. It collects pickup requests per tick (50, 100 or 600 ms) and runs them in a player rank that reshuffles every 60 s, not in arrival order. Ownership is checked at the tick that executes. Server truth view shows ids, owner dots, protection bars and the server panel.

2 · Ownership, party and death

The top damage dealer (the first attacker's damage counts x1.3) and their party own a corpse for 15 s. Anyone with 10% of the damage joins until 20 s, then it is public. Spills stay private 60 s, public until 180 s, and blink for the last 10 s. Bosses use personal loot sized by damage, damage taken and healing. In PvP the backpack drops at 100, 70, 45, 25, 10 or 0% by blessings, each worn slot at a tenth of that, everything on a red skull. Unskulled players keep their 3 best items, the amulet of loss saves all once. The body gives the killer's group and your party 60 s, then outsiders loot through a 1.5 s channel that damage restarts and wear a yellow LOOTER name for 2 min.

3 · Replication

Item adds (20 bytes) and removes (9 bytes) go only to players within 9 tiles, with a per-viewer owner flag. A corpse add is about 17 bytes, and its contents are sent when a player opens it. Gold on one tile merges every 2 s. With caps on the map keeps 200 ground items and evicts the cheapest first. At most 10 items draw per tile.

4 · Prediction

Cosmetic (recommended) plays a tick and a 2 px hop on click. The item stays on the ground until the server says OK, then flies. A rejection settles it with a 1 px shake and the reason. Wait for server shows dots until the answer. Full prediction flies the item at once and pops it back with "Sorry, not possible." when the server says no, which is why we do not recommend it. The race demo has you and a bot grab one item: an earlier tick wins, and inside one tick the rank decides.

5 · Drop presentation

Monsters keep their loot in the corpse, which glints in the best tier you may take. Bosses and death piles spill on a ring search, one item per tile, never on walls. Each item flies a 400 ms arc with a 10 px apex over a 60% floor shadow. Items are staggered 40 ms apart in tier order so the best lands last, and the whole sequence takes at most 600 ms. No bounce, except one 2 px hop for a legendary. Grabs open 400 ms after landing.

6 · Rarity

Common #E8E8E8, uncommon #4CE04C, rare #4C9BFF, epic #B45CFF, legendary #FF9A1F, each with a shape too. Uncommon gets corner pixels, rare a full outline, epic an outline and a sparkle, and legendary a beam. Rare and up pulse a halo sprite on a 2 s cycle capped at 60%. The legendary beam is 16 x 48 px. It stays for the owner until pickup and lasts 2 s for everyone else. Stings play for your own loot only.

7 · Labels and filters

Hold Alt for labels on the items you may take, capped at 32 and sorted by tier, then yours, then distance. The label filter picks which of them show. With Labels on, the filter applies all the time and Alt shows every label. Each label sits next to its item first, then steps up or down an 8 px grid up to 7 times. It never sits over a legendary item or its beam, and hidden labels leave a "+N". Labels are baked once into a cache and drawn above every item sprite. Naive render swaps in shadowBlur glows, per-glyph text, a new gradient per beam and an uncapped O(n²) solver.

8 · Pickup

Reach is 1 tile, and a farther click walks you there and retries every 300 ms. The gold magnet reaches 1 tile, only for your own gold, 400 ms after landing and never in PvP combat. The server grants it and the client pulls the coins in over 150 ms. E loots every corpse you may open in the 3x3 around you, up to 30. Items fly to the bag in 250 ms on a curve of up to 8 px, and the bag bumps to 1.15x over 120 ms. More than 3 items fly 3 sprites plus a "+N". Coin pitch rises a semitone per pickup within 1.2 s, up to 8.

9 · Crowd budget and access

Strangers' drops at reduced LOD have no arc, no glow, no labels and no sound, and their legendary beam lasts 2 s. Party drops keep arcs and glow but no sting. Caps hold glows at 24, beams at 8 and labels at 32, with about 200 visible ground items as the budget. Loot sounds share 8 voices, own before party before others, then by tier. A legendary sting ducks the loot bus 6 dB for 300 ms.
